# Business Context

## Project Name

Ca Heo DIY

---

## Overview

Ca Heo DIY is a personal ecommerce platform built and operated by a single owner.

The platform focuses on selling self-designed and self-manufactured products rather than reselling products from large suppliers.

This project is intentionally small, simple, and owner-operated.

---

## Business Goals

The primary goals are:

* Sell products online
* Showcase custom creations
* Accept customer orders
* Build a long-term independent brand
* Maintain a direct relationship with customers

The platform should remain easy to operate and maintain.

---

## Product Categories

Current and future products may include:

### 3D Printed Products

Examples:

* Home accessories
* Desk accessories
* Functional printed parts
* Decorative items
* Custom printed designs

### DIY Kits

Examples:

* Electronics kits
* Educational kits
* Maker projects
* Hobby projects

### Hydroponics and Gardening

Examples:

* Hydroponic accessories
* Plant holders
* Custom growing systems
* Garden tools

### Custom Orders

Products manufactured or modified according to customer requirements.

---

## Target Customers

Typical customers include:

* Makers
* DIY enthusiasts
* Engineering students
* Technology enthusiasts
* Hobbyists
* Home gardeners
* Customers looking for custom products

Most customers are expected to be located in Vietnam.

---

## Business Characteristics

The business is different from large ecommerce companies.

Characteristics:

* Small inventory
* Small product catalog
* Low operational complexity
* Many products may be produced on demand
* Product quality is more important than product quantity

The system should support these characteristics.

---

## Store Philosophy

Products are the center of the experience.

Customers should be able to:

1. Discover products easily
2. Understand products quickly
3. View photos clearly
4. Place orders with minimal friction
5. Contact the shop owner when necessary

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

## User Roles

### Guest

Can:

* Browse products
* Search products
* View product details

### Customer

Can:

* Manage account
* Manage shipping information
* Place orders
* View order history

### Administrator

Can:

* Manage products
* Manage categories
* Manage inventory
* Manage orders
* Manage website content

Keep permissions simple.

---

## Design Priorities

Priority order:

1. Simplicity
2. Mobile usability
3. Fast loading
4. Reliability
5. Ease of maintenance

The website should feel lightweight and easy to use.

---

## Non Goals

The following features are intentionally out of scope unless explicitly requested:

* Multi-vendor marketplace
* Seller dashboards
* Affiliate systems
* Loyalty point systems
* Auction systems
* Social networking features
* Livestream selling
* ERP integrations
* Complex warehouse management
* Microservice architecture
* Enterprise-scale solutions

Do not introduce these features without a specific requirement.

---

## Development Philosophy

Prefer:

* Phoenix conventions
* LiveView-first solutions
* Simple database models
* Maintainable code
* Incremental improvements

Avoid:

* Over-engineering
* Premature optimization
* Unnecessary abstractions
* Complex architectures

When multiple solutions exist, prefer the simpler solution.
